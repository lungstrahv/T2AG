> **Which text governs / 文本效力**: the **Chinese** text below is the governing version. The English text is a
> complete translation provided so that an invited grantee who does not read Chinese can know what they are

This grant is **distributed per release version**: one grant corresponds to one released version and ships with the
package. When the grantor publishes a new version, a separate grant is issued for it; a grantee who moves to the new
version thereafter relies on that version's grant. This file supersedes the 2026-08-10 "T2AG 0.2.3 Skeleton Trial
Licence (personal evaluation)", which remains effective for already-delivered copies until the grantee moves to this
file.

## 1. Who the grantee is

The grantee is **an individual invited by the grantor who received this version of the release and this file
directly from the grantor**. This grant does not extend to others by forwarding: anyone who has not received the
invitation and this file directly from the grantor is outside its scope, however they obtained a copy.

There is no cap on the number of grantees and no commitment to any number; the grantor invites people one at a time,
at their own pace.

## 2. Rights granted

While this grant has not been revoked, the grantee may:

1. Extract, copy and run this release on their own devices, or on lab devices they are entitled to use;
2. Freely modify their own copy for the purpose of their own use and evaluation;
3. Use this release for their own real learning, and keep all learning records arising from it (see §5).

## 3. Obligations of the grantee

As consideration for a free grant, the grantee shall:

1. **Give feedback** — during use, report to the grantor on usability, defects, misleading design and real obstacles
   encountered. The form is unrestricted (email, instant message, issue, spoken), with no required length or
   frequency; but the grantor may revoke a grant under this clause where feedback is entirely absent;
2. **Not misrepresent** — in any external description, do not present this release as a product, service or
   commitment provided by the grantor, and do not make any statement on the grantor's behalf.

Feedback content is treated as licensed by the grantee to the grantor, free of charge, for improving T2AG and its
public explanatory materials. When quoting specific feedback publicly, the grantor anonymizes it by default, unless
the grantee separately agrees to attribution.

## 4. Rights not granted

The following are outside this grant without the grantor's separate written consent:

1. **Redistribution** — do not pass this release or derivative copies to third parties, including public
   repositories, file-sharing drives, course materials or sharing inside an organization;
2. **Commercial use** — do not use it for paid teaching, consulting delivery, or any commercial activity whose
   subject matter is this release;
3. **Removing marks** — do not remove or rewrite the authorship attribution, the constitution preface signature, or
   the version markers inside the release.

## 5. The grantee's learning records belong to the grantee

Learning records the grantee produces through use — including the profile, course progress, activity ledgers,
mistake banks, classroom records and personal reflections — **belong to the grantee**. No right in them transfers to
the grantor under this grant. The grantor does not require uploading, does not require syncing, operates no remote
account, and does not request these records in any way.

This clause survives termination of the grant and is unaffected by revocation: after the grantee stops using the
release, the learning records already produced remain entirely theirs, to keep, migrate or delete freely.

## 6. Term and revocation

This grant is effective from the date of delivery until any of the following occurs:

1. Either party terminates in writing (including email or instant message);
2. The grantor revokes all grants for this version (all grantees will be notified at the same time).

After termination the grantee shall stop using it and delete their copies of the release. The learning records
described in §5 are not among the items to be deleted.

## 7. Disclaimer

This release is provided "AS IS", without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including but not limited to
merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. The grantor bears no liability for any
direct or indirect loss caused by use of this release.

This release is an **empty instance template**. It contains no real student data, no course progress and no
third-party textbook content. The legality of any textbook, paper or course material the grantee imports is the
grantee's own responsibility.

## 8. Relationship to a future open-source licence

T2AG is intended to continue as a reusable open-source foundation. The grantor's current plan is that, after the
trial experiment closes, the code layer will be published under the Apache License 2.0 and the prose layer (the
constitution, playbooks and so on) under CC BY-SA 4.0. **That plan has not been executed and is not a commitment**;
the grantor reserves the right to change both the licence choice and its timing.

This grant is a temporary grant to invited individuals. It is not an open-source licence, and it neither predetermines
nor restricts any licence the grantor may later choose (including a more permissive one). Once a formal licence is
published, the grantee may elect to rely on its terms instead.
